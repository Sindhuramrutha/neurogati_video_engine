Subtopic: 2.1.4 Components of the Acquisition Chain - Biological Source
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Let's go through each component. First, the biological source. Every recording begins with a biological event occurring inside the body. Depending on the recording modality, that source could be the electrical activity of cortical neurons, muscle fibers, eye muscles, or individual neurons. And here's the obvious but important point: without this physiological activity, there is simply no signal to record.
