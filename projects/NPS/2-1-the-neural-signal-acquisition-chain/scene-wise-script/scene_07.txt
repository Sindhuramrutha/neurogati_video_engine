Subtopic: 2.1.4 Components of the Acquisition Chain - ADC
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Up to this stage, everything we have is still an analog signal — a continuous voltage that varies smoothly in time. But computers can only process digital information. That's where the Analog-to-Digital Converter comes in. The ADC samples the analog signal at regular intervals, and it converts each sample into a numerical value. The result is a digital representation of the original biological signal: a sequence of numbers, not a continuous waveform.
