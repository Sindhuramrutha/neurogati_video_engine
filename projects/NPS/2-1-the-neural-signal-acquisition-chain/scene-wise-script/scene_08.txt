Subtopic: 2.1.4 Components of the Acquisition Chain - Computer
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Finally, once the signal is digitized, it's transferred to the computer. Specialized software displays, stores, processes, and analyzes the recorded data. And here's an important point: everything that comes after this stage — filtering, feature extraction, spectral analysis, classification — all of it depends on the quality of the signal that has been acquired up to this point. The computer can only work with what it's been given.
