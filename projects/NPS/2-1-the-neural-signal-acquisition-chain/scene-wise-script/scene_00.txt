Subtopic: 2.1.1 Introduction
Scene type: manim

Narration:
In the previous module, we saw how neurons generate electrical signals, and how those signals can be recorded at different spatial scales. But here's the problem: neural activity is happening inside the body. You can't just plug a brain into a computer and watch the waveform appear. So the question is: how does a tiny biological signal generated inside the brain actually become the waveform we see on a computer screen? The answer is a sequence of recording and processing steps, and together those steps are called the neural signal acquisition chain. And here's the key point: whether you're recording EEG, EMG, ECoG, or microelectrode signals, every recording system follows this same fundamental pathway. Understanding this chain gives us the foundation for everything else in this module.
