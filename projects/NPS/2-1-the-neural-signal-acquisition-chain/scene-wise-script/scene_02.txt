Subtopic: 2.1.3 The Neural Signal Acquisition Chain
Scene type: manim

Narration:
So what exactly is the acquisition chain? It's the complete sequence of hardware and electronic components that convert biological activity into digital data. Now, different recording techniques use different sensors, but the overall workflow stays the same. Here's the chain: Biological Source, then Electrode or Sensor, then Amplifier, then Filter, then Analog-to-Digital Converter, and finally the Computer. Each component performs a specific function, and the quality of the final recording depends on every single stage working successfully. If one stage fails, the whole recording suffers.
