Subtopic: 2.1.4 Components of the Acquisition Chain - Electrode or Sensor
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Next comes the electrode or sensor. This is the device that detects the biological signal and transfers it to the recording system. Different techniques use different sensing devices. For example, EEG uses scalp electrodes placed on the head. EMG uses surface or needle electrodes. ECoG uses electrode grids placed directly on the cortical surface. And MEG uses specialized magnetic sensors. Now, their designs are quite different, but they all perform the same fundamental task: detecting physiological activity.
