Subtopic: 2.1.6 Summary
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Let's wrap up. In this section, we introduced the neural signal acquisition chain and examined each of its major components. The most important point to remember is that every neural recording system follows the same fundamental workflow: Biological Source, Electrode or Sensor, Amplifier, Filter, Analog-to-Digital Converter, and Computer. Although the sensing technology may differ across EEG, EMG, ECoG, or MEG, the overall acquisition process remains essentially the same.
