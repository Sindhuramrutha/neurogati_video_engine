Subtopic: 2.1.4 Components of the Acquisition Chain - Amplifier
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Now the amplifier. The biological signal detected by the electrode is extremely small — often only a few microvolts. That's too weak for any electronic system to process reliably. So we pass the signal through an amplifier. The amplifier increases the signal's amplitude, making it larger, while preserving the information contained within the waveform. The shape of the waveform stays the same; it just becomes bigger.
