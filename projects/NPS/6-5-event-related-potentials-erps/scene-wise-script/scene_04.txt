Subtopic: Applications of ERP Analysis
Scene type: image_only

Narration:
ERP analysis is widely used because it provides excellent temporal resolution, allowing researchers to study how rapidly the brain responds to events. Some common applications include: sensory processing — studying the brain's response to visual, auditory, or tactile stimuli; cognitive neuroscience — investigating attention, perception, memory, language, and decision-making; clinical research — examining changes in neural processing associated with neurological or psychiatric disorders; and brain–computer interfaces — detecting stimulus-related brain responses, such as the P300, for communication and control applications.

Second, a card with a brain icon and thought bubbles, labelled 'Cognitive Neuroscience'. Third, a card with a medical cross icon, labelled 'Clinical Research'. Fourth, a card showing a brain connected to a computer or a communication device, labelled 'Brain–Computer Interfaces'. Each card appears with a brief highlight as the narration mentions it.
