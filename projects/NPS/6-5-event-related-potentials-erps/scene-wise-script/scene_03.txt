Subtopic: ERP Components
Scene type: matplotlib

Narration:
An ERP waveform consists of a series of positive and negative voltage deflections. These deflections are referred to as ERP components. They are usually named according to their polarity — P for positive, N for negative — and their approximate latency after the stimulus. For example, P100 is a positive peak occurring approximately 100 milliseconds after stimulus onset. N100 is a negative peak occurring approximately 100 milliseconds after stimulus onset. P300 is a positive peak occurring approximately 300 milliseconds after stimulus onset. Different ERP components are associated with different stages of sensory, perceptual, or cognitive processing.

Each label appears as the narration mentions it, with a small arrow pointing to the corresponding peak.
