Subtopic: 2.2.4 Surface Electrodes – Advantages and Limitations
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Although surface electrodes are convenient, they are positioned some distance away from the actual biological source. As a result, the recorded signal is usually weaker and has lower spatial resolution than recordings obtained using more invasive electrodes. Their advantages are that they are non-invasive, comfortable for the subject, easy to apply, and suitable for long-duration recordings. Their limitations are lower signal amplitude, more susceptibility to motion artifacts, and lower spatial resolution.
