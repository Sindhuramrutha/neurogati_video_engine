Subtopic: 2.2.1 Introduction
Scene type: manim

Narration:
In the previous section, we learned that every neural recording system follows the same acquisition chain. The first hardware component in that chain is the electrode or sensor. Whether we're recording EEG, EMG, ECoG, or MEG, the recording process cannot begin unless the physiological signal is first detected. The device responsible for detecting that signal is the electrode or sensor. In this section, we'll look at the different types of electrodes and sensors used in neuroscience, understand how they differ from one another, and discuss why different recording techniques require different sensing technologies.
