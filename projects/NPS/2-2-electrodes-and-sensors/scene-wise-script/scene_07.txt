Subtopic: 2.2.5 The Fundamental Trade-off
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Here's an important note. As the recording electrode moves closer to the biological source, signal quality generally improves. However, increasing proximity usually increases invasiveness. This is one of the fundamental trade-offs in neural recording.
