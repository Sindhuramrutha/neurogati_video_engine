Subtopic: 2.2.6 EEG Electrodes
Scene type: manim

Narration:
EEG records electrical activity from the scalp using surface electrodes. These electrodes are positioned according to standardized placement systems, most commonly the International 10–20 System. Since EEG signals must travel through the meninges, skull, and scalp before reaching the electrodes, they are relatively small in amplitude. For this reason, maintaining low electrode impedance and good contact with the scalp is essential for obtaining high-quality EEG recordings. A detailed discussion of the 10–20 system will be covered later when EEG is discussed in the applications module.
