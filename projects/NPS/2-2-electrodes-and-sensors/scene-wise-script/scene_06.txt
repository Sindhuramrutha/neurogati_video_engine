Subtopic: 2.2.5 Microelectrodes
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Microelectrodes are extremely small electrodes designed for recording neural activity at the cellular level. They are capable of recording action potentials from individual neurons or very small groups of neurons. These electrodes are widely used in neuroscience research, neurophysiology, and brain–computer interface studies where high spatial resolution is required. Because microelectrodes penetrate neural tissue, they require surgical implantation and are therefore highly invasive.
