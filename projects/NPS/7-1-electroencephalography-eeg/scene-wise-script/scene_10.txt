Subtopic: 7.1.3 Introduction to Sleep Staging
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Sleep is one of the most important physiological processes of the human body. Although we appear inactive during sleep, the brain remains highly active, and its electrical activity changes continuously throughout the night. These changes are reflected in the EEG signal. By analysing characteristic patterns in the EEG, researchers and clinicians can determine the stage of sleep a person is experiencing at any given time. This process is known as sleep staging. Sleep staging is one of the most common clinical applications of EEG and plays an important role in sleep research and the diagnosis of sleep disorders.
