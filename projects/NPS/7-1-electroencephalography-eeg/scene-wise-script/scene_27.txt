Subtopic: 7.1.5 BCI Applications
Scene type: manim

Narration:
EEG-based BCIs are being developed for a wide range of applications. Assistive communication: BCIs enable individuals with severe motor impairments to communicate using brain activity alone. Wheelchair control: users can control powered wheelchairs through EEG-based commands. Prosthetic device control: brain signals can be used to operate robotic arms or prosthetic limbs. Neurorehabilitation: BCIs support rehabilitation by providing feedback during motor recovery following neurological injury. Research and human-computer interaction: EEG-based BCIs are increasingly used to study human cognition and develop new forms of interaction between humans and computers.
