Subtopic: 7.1.1 Connecting EEG to Neural Signal Processing
Scene type: manim

Narration:
It's important to recognize that EEG applications do not rely solely on the recorded waveform. Instead, meaningful information is extracted through signal processing. For example: filters remove unwanted electrical interference; spectral analysis identifies dominant frequency bands; spectrograms reveal how neural oscillations change over time; wavelet analysis detects transient neural events; and event-related potential analysis measures the brain's response to specific stimuli. Thus, every application discussed in this section builds upon the concepts developed throughout the earlier modules of this course.
