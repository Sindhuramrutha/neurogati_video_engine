Subtopic: 7.1.1 Summary and Transition to Brain States
Scene type: manim

Narration:
EEG is far more than a method for recording electrical activity from the scalp. It is a versatile tool that enables researchers and clinicians to study brain function, diagnose neurological disorders, monitor physiological states, and develop assistive technologies. In the following sections, we will examine four important applications of EEG and understand how neural signal processing enables each of these applications. One of the most fundamental applications of EEG is the study of brain states. The electrical activity of the brain changes continuously depending on whether a person is awake, relaxed, concentrating, or asleep. By analysing these changes, researchers can gain valuable insights into normal brain function and behaviour. In the next section, we will examine how EEG is used to study different brain states.
