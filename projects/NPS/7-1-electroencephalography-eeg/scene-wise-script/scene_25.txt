Subtopic: 7.1.5 How Does an EEG-Based BCI Work?
Scene type: manim

Narration:
An EEG-based BCI consists of several processing stages. Step 1: Signal Acquisition. EEG electrodes record the electrical activity of the brain. Step 2: Preprocessing. Filtering and artefact removal reduce unwanted noise and improve signal quality. Step 3: Feature Extraction. Relevant characteristics of the EEG signal are extracted. Examples include frequency-band power, event-related potentials such as the P300, time-frequency features, and spatial features. Step 4: Classification. Machine learning algorithms analyse the extracted features and identify the user's intended action. Step 5: Device Control. The classified output is converted into commands that control an external device.
