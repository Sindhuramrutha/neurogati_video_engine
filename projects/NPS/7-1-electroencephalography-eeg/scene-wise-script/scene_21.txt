Subtopic: 7.1.4 Clinical Applications and Advantages of EEG in Epilepsy
Scene type: manim

Narration:
EEG plays an important role throughout the management of epilepsy. Diagnosis: EEG helps identify abnormal electrical activity that supports the diagnosis of epilepsy. Seizure classification: different seizure types produce different EEG patterns, helping clinicians classify seizures and guide treatment decisions. Monitoring treatment: repeated EEG recordings can be used to monitor disease progression and evaluate the effectiveness of anti-seizure medications. Presurgical evaluation: for patients whose seizures cannot be controlled with medication, EEG contributes to identifying the region of the brain where seizures originate, and this information may be combined with other imaging techniques during surgical planning. EEG offers several important advantages for epilepsy evaluation: direct measurement of brain electrical activity, excellent temporal resolution, non-invasive and widely available, useful for long-term monitoring, and supports both visual interpretation and automated analysis. A normal EEG does not necessarily rule out epilepsy. Some individuals with epilepsy may have normal EEG recordings between seizures. For this reason, EEG findings are always interpreted together with the patient's clinical history, observed symptoms, and, when necessary, additional diagnostic investigations.
