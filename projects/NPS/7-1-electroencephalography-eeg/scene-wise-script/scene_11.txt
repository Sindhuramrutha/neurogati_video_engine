Subtopic: 7.1.3 What is Sleep Staging?
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Sleep staging is the process of identifying different stages of sleep by analysing physiological signals, particularly the EEG. Sleep is not a single, uniform state. Instead, it consists of a sequence of stages, each characterized by distinct patterns of brain activity. As a person progresses from wakefulness to deep sleep and eventually to Rapid Eye Movement, or REM sleep, the electrical activity recorded by EEG changes in a predictable manner. These characteristic EEG patterns allow clinicians to determine the current stage of sleep. Because EEG directly reflects cortical activity with high temporal resolution, it is considered one of the most important signals used in sleep analysis.
