Subtopic: 7.1.5 Introduction to BCIs
Scene type: manim

Narration:
So far, we have explored how EEG is used to observe and understand brain activity in applications such as brain-state monitoring, sleep staging, and epilepsy diagnosis. In these applications, EEG serves as a tool for measuring neural activity. However, EEG can also be used in a very different way. Instead of simply recording brain activity, EEG signals can be translated into commands that allow a person to interact with external devices. This technology is known as a Brain-Computer Interface, or BCI. A Brain-Computer Interface establishes a direct communication pathway between the brain and an external system, enabling users to control computers, wheelchairs, robotic devices, or communication systems using their brain activity.
