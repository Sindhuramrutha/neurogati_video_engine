Narration:
At this stage, the neural signal has been detected, amplified, and conditioned. However, it is still an analogue signal. Since computers can process only digital information, the next step is to convert this continuous electrical signal into a sequence of numerical values. In the next section, we will discuss sampling and analog-to-digital conversion, or ADC, and understand how analogue neural signals become digital data ready for computer analysis.
