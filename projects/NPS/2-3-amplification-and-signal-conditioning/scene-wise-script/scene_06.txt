Subtopic: 2.3.7 Practical Considerations
Scene type: manim

Narration:
When designing a neural recording system, amplification and signal conditioning must be performed carefully. Too little amplification may produce a signal that is difficult to analyse. Excessive amplification, however, may also amplify unwanted noise or even saturate the recording system. Similarly, poor signal conditioning may remove useful physiological information or allow unwanted interference to remain in the recording. For this reason, amplification and signal conditioning are considered critical stages in neural signal acquisition.
