Subtopic: 2.3.6 Amplification and Signal Conditioning in the Acquisition Chain
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Let us return to the neural signal acquisition chain: biological source, electrode, amplifier, signal conditioning, ADC, computer. Once the electrode detects the biological signal, the amplifier increases its strength. The conditioned signal is then cleaned and prepared for digitisation. Only after these steps is the signal suitable for conversion into digital data by the analog-to-digital converter. Understanding this sequence is important because later stages of the acquisition chain depend heavily on the quality of the amplified and conditioned signal.
