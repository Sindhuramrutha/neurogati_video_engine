Subtopic: 2.3.8 Summary
Scene type: manim

Narration:
In this section, we examined the role of amplification and signal conditioning in neural signal acquisition. Neural signals are extremely weak and therefore require amplification before further processing. Signal conditioning prepares the amplified signal for digitisation by reducing unwanted interference and ensuring that the signal entering the ADC is suitable for accurate digital conversion. Together, amplification and signal conditioning improve the reliability of the recorded signal and support accurate downstream analysis.
