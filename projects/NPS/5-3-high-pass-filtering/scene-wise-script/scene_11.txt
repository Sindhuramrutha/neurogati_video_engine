Subtopic: Limitations
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Although high-pass filtering is useful, it should be applied with care. If the cutoff frequency is chosen too high, slow physiological components that matter for your analysis may also be removed. For example, excessively high cutoff frequencies can distort slow EEG activity, or alter the shape of event-related potentials. So the cutoff frequency should always be selected according to the characteristics of the recorded signal and the objectives of the analysis.
