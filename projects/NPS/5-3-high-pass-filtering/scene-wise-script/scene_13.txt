Narration:
So to summarize: we introduced the high-pass filter and its role in neural signal processing. A high-pass filter preserves high-frequency components while reducing frequencies below a selected cutoff frequency. It is commonly used to reduce baseline drift, suppress slow movement artefacts, and improve the visibility of rapidly changing neural activity. And like all filters, it must be configured carefully to avoid removing useful physiological information.
