Subtopic: What is a High-Pass Filter? - Intuition
Scene type: manim

Narration:
As the name suggests, a high-pass filter does the opposite of a low-pass filter. Instead of preserving slow signal variations, it removes them and allows faster signal variations to remain. Imagine a gate that lets only fast-moving vehicles through while stopping the slower ones. In just the same way, a high-pass filter allows high-frequency signal components to continue through the system while reducing lower-frequency components. That makes this filter especially useful when the unwanted information lives primarily at low frequencies.
