Subtopic: The Concept of Cutoff Frequency
Scene type: matplotlib

Narration:
Just like the low-pass filter, a high-pass filter is defined by a cutoff frequency. The cutoff frequency is the frequency below which signal components begin to be attenuated by the filter. To make this concrete, suppose a high-pass filter has a cutoff frequency of one hertz. Signal components above one hertz are largely preserved. Components below one hertz are progressively attenuated. And, as with the low-pass filter, this transition is gradual, not abrupt. Frequencies close to the cutoff are only partially attenuated, while frequencies much lower than the cutoff experience much greater attenuation.
