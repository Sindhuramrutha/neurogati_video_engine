Subtopic: Transition to Section 5.4
Scene type: manim

Narration:
So far, we have studied two fundamental filters. A low-pass filter preserves low frequencies, while a high-pass filter preserves high frequencies. But many neural signals occupy only a specific range of frequencies. In those situations, we might want to remove both very low and very high frequencies, while preserving only the frequencies of interest. To do that, we use a band-pass filter — and that will be the focus of the next section.
