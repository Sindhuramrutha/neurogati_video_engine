Subtopic: Important Note
Scene type: manim

Narration:
One important note: a high-pass filter removes low-frequency components, not simply noise. If useful physiological information exists below the selected cutoff frequency, that information will also be attenuated. Filter settings should therefore be chosen based on the characteristics of the biological signal, rather than using a fixed value for every application.
