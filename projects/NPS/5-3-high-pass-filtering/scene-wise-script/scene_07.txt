Subtopic: Applications in Neural Signal Processing: EEG
Scene type: manim

Narration:
High-pass filters are used widely in biomedical signal processing to remove slow variations that interfere with signal interpretation. In EEG, high-pass filters help reduce slow baseline drift caused by electrode movement or changes in electrode impedance, making the recordings more stable for analysis.
