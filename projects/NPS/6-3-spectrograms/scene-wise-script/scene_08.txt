Subtopic: Transition to Wavelet Analysis
Scene type: manim

Narration:
The spectrogram represents a major improvement over conventional frequency analysis, because it shows both when and at what frequency neural activity occurs. But it still depends on the fixed analysis window used by the Short-Time Fourier Transform. This fixed window creates a compromise between time resolution and frequency resolution. Is there a method that can adapt its analysis to different types of neural activity? The answer is wavelet analysis, which we will introduce in the next section.
