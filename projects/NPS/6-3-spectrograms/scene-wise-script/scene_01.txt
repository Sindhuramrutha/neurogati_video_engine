Subtopic: What is a Spectrogram?
Scene type: manim

Narration:
So what exactly is a spectrogram? A spectrogram is a graphical representation of how the frequency content of a signal changes over time. It is generated by computing the Short-Time Fourier Transform over consecutive segments of the signal. Instead of displaying only one frequency spectrum, a spectrogram combines the spectra from all STFT windows into a single image. This allows us to observe both when specific frequencies occur and how their strengths change over time. In short, a spectrogram transforms a complex sequence of frequency spectra into a visual map that is much easier to interpret. For this reason, spectrograms are widely used in speech processing, biomedical engineering, and neuroscience.
