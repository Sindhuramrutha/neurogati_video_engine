Subtopic: Applications in Neural Signal Processing
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Spectrograms are widely used in neural signal processing because many neural signals vary continuously over time. Some common applications include EEG analysis, where spectrograms help visualise changes in neural oscillations during cognitive tasks, sleep, or sensory stimulation. They are also used in EMG analysis, where they help observe how muscle activation changes during movement or fatigue. In brain–computer interfaces, spectrograms are used to monitor time-varying neural rhythms that are needed for feature extraction and classification. And in event-related studies, spectrograms help examine changes in neural activity before, during, and after a stimulus is presented.
