Subtopic: Safety and Signal Quality
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Electrical safety and signal quality are actually closely related. Poor electrode connections, damaged cables, or improperly configured equipment can degrade the recorded signal — and they can also threaten the safe operation of the system. For this reason, recording systems should always be checked before data acquisition begins. Routine inspection and proper equipment maintenance aren't optional extras; they're essential parts of good laboratory practice.
