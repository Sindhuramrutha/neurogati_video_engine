Subtopic: What is Electrical Isolation?
Scene type: manim

Narration:
One of the most effective ways to protect participants is electrical isolation. Electrical isolation separates the participant from potentially hazardous electrical circuits, while still allowing physiological signals to be recorded. The principle is actually straightforward. The biological signal is allowed to pass from the participant to the recording system, but direct electrical pathways that could carry hazardous currents are blocked. In other words, isolation acts as a protective barrier between the participant and the powered electronic equipment. Modern biomedical recording systems incorporate isolation as a standard safety feature, so neural signals can be recorded without compromising participant safety.
