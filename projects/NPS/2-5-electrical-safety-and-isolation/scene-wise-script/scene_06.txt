Narration:
And with that, we've completed this module on neural signal acquisition. Let's take a moment to recap the whole journey. We began with the acquisition chain, exploring how biological signals are detected by electrodes and sensors, strengthened through amplification, prepared by signal conditioning, converted into digital data using an analog-to-digital converter, and finally protected through appropriate electrical safety measures. The key concepts are: the acquisition chain provides the complete pathway from biological activity to digital data. Different recording modalities use different electrodes or sensors depending on the application. Amplification increases the strength of weak biological signals. Signal conditioning prepares the signal for accurate digitisation. Sampling and ADC convert continuous analogue signals into digital data suitable for computer analysis. And electrical safety and isolation are essential to protect both participants and recording equipment. Together, these concepts form the foundation of neural signal acquisition. They prepare us for the upcoming modules, where we'll explore preprocessing techniques, filtering, frequency-domain analysis, and applications of neural signal processing.
