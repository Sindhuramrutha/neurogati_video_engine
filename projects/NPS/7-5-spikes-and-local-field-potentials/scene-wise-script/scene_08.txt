Subtopic: Role of LFPs in Neural Coding
Scene type: manim

Narration:
LFPs provide another perspective on neural coding. Rather than focusing on individual spikes, LFP analysis can reveal the collective activity and oscillatory dynamics of nearby neural populations. Thus, combining spike and LFP analysis can help researchers investigate neural coding at both the individual-neuron level and the population level.
