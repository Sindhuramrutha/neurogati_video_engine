Subtopic: Detecting and Isolating Spikes
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Because spikes are small and brief, they can be difficult to distinguish from noise. An extracellular recording can contain neural spikes, background neural activity, electronic noise, thermal noise, and other physiological signals. One common approach is to separate the high-frequency spike component from slower activity. For example, a high-pass filter around 300 hertz can be used to isolate spike-related activity from the slower LFP component. Once spikes have been isolated, researchers can identify individual spike events.
