Subtopic: Circuit Analysis: Multiple Recording Sites
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Neurons rarely operate in isolation. Brain functions emerge from interactions between many neurons and neural populations. This is where circuit analysis becomes important. With multiple recording contacts, researchers can record neural activity from different locations simultaneously. This allows them to compare spike activity, LFP activity, timing, oscillations, and relationships between different brain regions. Multiple recording locations allow researchers to study relationships between local populations and investigate network-level phenomena.
