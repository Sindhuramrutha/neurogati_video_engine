Subtopic: Circuit Analysis: Synchronization and Phase
Scene type: manim

Narration:
One example of circuit analysis is examining whether oscillations recorded from two locations are synchronized. Suppose two brain regions show oscillations at a similar frequency. If their oscillations are also consistently aligned in phase, this may indicate a functional relationship between the two regions. This kind of analysis cannot be obtained from a single recording contact. Therefore, multiple electrodes allow us to move from local neural activity to relationships between populations to neural circuit analysis.
