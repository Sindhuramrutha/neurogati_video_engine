Subtopic: 5.6.1 Introduction
Scene type: manim

Narration:
In earlier sections we explored filters that change the frequency content of a signal, like low‑pass, high‑pass, and band‑pass filters. But not all filtering is done in the frequency domain. Some filters work directly on the signal waveform in the time domain, modifying it sample by sample. These time‑domain filters are simple to implement and very useful for reducing random fluctuations and impulsive noise. The two most widely used are the moving‑average filter and the median filter. Let’s see how they work, compare them, and understand when to use each.
