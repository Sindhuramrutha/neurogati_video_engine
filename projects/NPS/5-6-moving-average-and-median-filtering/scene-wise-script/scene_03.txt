Subtopic: 5.6.3 Moving‑Average Filter – Example
Scene type: manim

Narration:
For example, suppose we average every five consecutive samples. If one sample has a small random spike, averaging it with its neighbours reduces its influence. When you do this throughout a recording, the result is a much smoother waveform, as shown in the typical figure 5.6A. The original noisy signal becomes visibly less jagged.

Animation description:
Show an original noisy waveform (random high‑frequency noise). Apply a moving average with window size 5 over the entire signal. Overlay the original noisy trace and the newly smoothed trace, and visually highlight the reduction in fluctuation. Optionally display a label ‘Window size = 5’.
