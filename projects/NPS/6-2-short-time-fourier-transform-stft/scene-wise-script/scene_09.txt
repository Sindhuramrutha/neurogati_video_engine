Subtopic: 6.2.6 Important Concept — Fixed window trade-off
Scene type: manim

Narration:
This creates a fundamental trade-off. A small window tells us when something happened more accurately. A large window tells us which frequency is present more accurately. Unfortunately, a single fixed window cannot optimise both simultaneously. STFT uses a fixed window size. This means that the same window is used throughout the entire signal, regardless of whether the signal changes rapidly or slowly. As we will see later, this fixed-window approach is one of the major limitations of STFT.
