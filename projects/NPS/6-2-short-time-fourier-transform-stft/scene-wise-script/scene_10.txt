Subtopic: 6.2.7 Applications in Neural Signal Processing
Scene type: image_only

Narration:
The Short-Time Fourier Transform is widely used because many neural signals change continuously over time. Some common applications include: EEG, for tracking how neural oscillations change during cognitive tasks, sleep, or sensory stimulation. EMG, for observing changes in muscle activity during movement and fatigue. Event-related neural activity, for examining frequency changes before, during, and after stimulus presentation. And brain–computer interfaces, for monitoring time-varying neural rhythms used for feature extraction and classification.
