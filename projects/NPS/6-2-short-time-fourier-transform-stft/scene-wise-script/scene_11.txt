Subtopic: 6.2.8 Advantages
Scene type: image_only

Narration:
The Short-Time Fourier Transform provides several important advantages. It describes both time and frequency. It is suitable for analysing nonstationary signals. It is relatively simple to implement. And it forms the basis for spectrogram generation.
