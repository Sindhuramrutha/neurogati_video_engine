Subtopic: 2.4.7 Importance of Proper Sampling
Scene type: manim

Narration:
The quality of the digital recording depends greatly on how the signal is sampled. If too few samples are collected, important variations in the neural signal may not be represented accurately. Conversely, collecting more samples than necessary increases storage requirements and processing time without always providing additional useful information. So selecting an appropriate sampling frequency is an important consideration during the design of every neural recording system. And here's why it matters downstream: the accuracy of later analyses — like filtering, spectral analysis, and feature extraction — depends on the quality of the digital data produced during the sampling stage. A poorly digitised signal cannot be fully corrected during later processing.
