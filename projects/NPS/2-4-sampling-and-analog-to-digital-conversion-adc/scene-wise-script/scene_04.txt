Subtopic: 2.4.5 Sampling Frequency
Scene type: manim

Narration:
The sampling frequency — also called the sampling rate — is the number of samples collected every second. It's measured in Hertz. Sampling frequency determines how frequently the analogue signal is measured. A sampling frequency of 250 Hz means 250 samples per second. 500 Hz means 500 samples per second. 1000 Hz means 1000 samples per second. Higher sampling frequencies provide more detailed information about the signal, because more measurements are taken over the same period of time. However, higher sampling rates also increase data storage requirements and computational cost. So selecting an appropriate sampling frequency means balancing recording accuracy with efficient data processing. The key point: the sampling frequency must be sufficiently high to capture the important characteristics of the biological signal without losing information.
