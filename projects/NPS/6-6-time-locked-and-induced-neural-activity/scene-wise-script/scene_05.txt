Narration:
To summarize, we distinguished between time-locked and induced neural activity. Time-locked responses occur at approximately the same latency after repeated events, and therefore become clearer through ERP averaging. In contrast, induced neural activity is related to the event but varies in timing across trials. Because averaging reduces these responses, they are typically investigated using time-frequency analysis methods such as the STFT, spectrograms, and wavelet analysis. Understanding this distinction is essential for selecting appropriate analytical techniques and correctly interpreting neural recordings.
