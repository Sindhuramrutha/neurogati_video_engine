Subtopic: 7.7.7 Capstone Activity
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Now it is your turn to apply the concepts from the entire course. Choose one application: sleep staging, epilepsy detection, movement analysis, or brain-computer interface. Then design a simple processing pipeline. For example, for sleep staging: start with raw EEG, then preprocessing to remove artifacts and apply appropriate filtering, then feature extraction to calculate frequency-band power, then classification to classify the signal into a sleep stage, then output: wake, NREM, or REM. The important part of this activity is not choosing the most advanced algorithm. The goal is to understand why each stage is necessary and how the stages connect together.
