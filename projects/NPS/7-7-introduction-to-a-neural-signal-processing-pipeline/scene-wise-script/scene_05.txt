Subtopic: 7.7.6 Complete Neural Signal-Processing Pipeline
Scene type: manim

Narration:
We can now combine all the stages into a complete pipeline: raw neural signal, then preprocessing, then feature extraction, then interpretation or classification. This same general framework can be applied to many neural-signal problems. For example, for sleep staging: raw EEG, then filtering, then frequency features, then classification, then sleep stage. For epilepsy detection: raw EEG, then preprocessing, then relevant signal features, then classification, then seizure or non-seizure. For movement analysis: raw EMG, then filtering, then amplitude and frequency features, then interpretation or classification, then movement information. For brain-computer interface: raw EEG, then preprocessing, then neural features, then classification, then control command. The exact algorithms and features change depending on the problem, but the overall processing logic remains similar.
