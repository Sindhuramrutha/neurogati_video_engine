Subtopic: 7.7.8 Summary
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Let's summarize. A neural signal-processing pipeline provides a systematic way to transform a raw neural recording into meaningful information. The basic pipeline is: raw signal, preprocessing, feature extraction, then interpretation or classification. We learned that the raw signal contains the recorded neural activity along with unwanted components. Preprocessing prepares the signal for analysis. Feature extraction converts the signal into meaningful quantitative measurements. Interpretation helps us understand neural activity, while classification assigns neural activity to predefined categories or commands. The same general framework can be adapted to different neuroscience applications.
