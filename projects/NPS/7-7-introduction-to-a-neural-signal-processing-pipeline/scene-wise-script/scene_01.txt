Subtopic: 7.7.2 Stage 1: Raw Neural Signal
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Let's start with stage one: the raw neural signal. The raw signal is the neural recording obtained directly from the recording system before substantial processing or analysis. Depending on the application, the raw signal could be EEG, ECoG, EMG, EOG, spike recordings, LFP, or MEG. For example, an EEG system may record voltage continuously from multiple scalp electrodes. At this stage, the signal may contain both the neural activity we are interested in and unwanted components such as noise and physiological artifacts. Therefore, the raw recording is usually not ready for direct interpretation.
