Subtopic: 5.4.1 Introduction
Scene type: manim

Narration:
In the previous sections, we studied two fundamental filters: low‑pass and high‑pass. A low‑pass filter removes high frequencies, and a high‑pass filter removes low frequencies. But many neural signals are concentrated in a specific frequency band. For those, neither filter alone is enough. We need a filter that preserves only a selected range of frequencies, while removing both lower and higher frequencies outside that range. That’s called a band‑pass filter. Band‑pass filtering is one of the most common preprocessing steps in neural signal processing because many physiological signals are analysed in specific frequency bands.
