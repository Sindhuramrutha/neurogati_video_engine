Subtopic: 5.4.4 Lower and Upper Cutoff Frequencies
Scene type: manim

Narration:
A band‑pass filter is defined by two cutoff frequencies. The lower cutoff frequency determines where the filter begins to preserve the signal – frequencies below this value are attenuated. The upper cutoff frequency determines where the filter begins to suppress higher‑frequency components – frequencies above this value are attenuated. For example, if we set the lower cutoff to 1 hertz and the upper cutoff to 40 hertz, then frequencies below 1 hertz are reduced, frequencies between 1 and 40 hertz are preserved, and frequencies above 40 hertz are reduced. The resulting signal contains only the frequency components within the selected band.
