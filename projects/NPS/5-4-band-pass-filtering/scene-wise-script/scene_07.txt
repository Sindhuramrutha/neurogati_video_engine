Subtopic: 5.4.8 Limitations
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Although band‑pass filtering is highly effective, the choice of cutoff frequencies is critical. If the selected frequency band is too narrow, useful physiological information may be lost. If the band is too wide, unwanted components may remain in the recording. Therefore, cutoff frequencies should always be selected according to the characteristics of the signal and the objectives of the analysis. Remember, a band‑pass filter does not automatically improve every recording. Its effectiveness depends on choosing an appropriate frequency range for the physiological signal being studied. Choosing incorrect cutoff frequencies may remove useful information or preserve unwanted interference.
