Subtopic: 5.4.7 Advantages
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Band‑pass filtering offers several important advantages. It removes both low‑frequency and high‑frequency interference, improves the signal‑to‑noise ratio, preserves only the frequency range relevant to the analysis, and is widely applicable across different neural recording modalities.
