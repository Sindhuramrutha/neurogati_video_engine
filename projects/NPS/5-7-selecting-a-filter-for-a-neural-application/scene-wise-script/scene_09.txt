Subtopic: 5.7.5 Selecting Filters for Common Neural Signals
Scene type: manim

Narration:
The right filter also depends on the recording modality. For example, EEG, EMG, local field potentials, and single-unit spikes all have different frequency bands of interest. The exact filter settings must always be chosen based on your study's objectives and the characteristics of the recorded data.
