Subtopic: 5.7.4 Choosing the Appropriate Filter – Notch
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Use a notch filter when your recording suffers from power-line interference or another narrow-band frequency. Typical applications are removing 50 Hz or 60 Hz interference from the mains supply.

Animation description:
A frequency spectrum of a signal is shown with a tall narrow peak at 50 Hz. A notch filter transfer function is overlaid: a deep narrow dip at exactly 50 Hz. The spectrum updates to show the peak removed. In the time domain, a signal with a visible 50 Hz hum is seen, then after filtering the hum disappears and the neural signal emerges cleanly. Text '50 Hz interference removed' flashes.
