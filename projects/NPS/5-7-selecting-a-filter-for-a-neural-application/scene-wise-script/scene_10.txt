Subtopic: 5.7.6 Common Mistakes During Filter Selection
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Picking the wrong filter can degrade your signal. Common mistakes include: applying filters without first identifying the noise source, choosing cutoff frequencies that accidentally remove useful physiological information, over-filtering to the point of distorting waveforms, using a notch filter when there's no power-line interference present, and assuming one filter is suitable for every recording. Filtering should always be done with a clear objective. The goal is not to produce the smoothest possible waveform; it's to preserve the information that matters for your analysis while reducing unwanted interference.
