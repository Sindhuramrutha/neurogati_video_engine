Subtopic: 5.7.1 Introduction
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Throughout this module, we've studied several filter types: low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, notch, moving-average, and median filters. Each one was built to tackle a different problem. That raises a key question: when we have multiple filters in our toolbox, how do we pick the right one for a given neural recording? The answer rests on a simple principle: the choice must be driven by the characteristics of the recorded signal and the goal of the analysis. There is no universal filter that fits every application. Selecting the correct filter means understanding both the physiological signal we care about and the unwanted components that contaminate the recording.
