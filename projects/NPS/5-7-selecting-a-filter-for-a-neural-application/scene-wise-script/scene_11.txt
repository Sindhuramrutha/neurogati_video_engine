Subtopic: 5.7.7 Summary
Scene type: manim

Narration:
In this section, we discussed how to select an appropriate filter for neural signal processing. Filter selection hinges on the physiological signal you're recording, the type of unwanted interference, the frequency range containing useful information, and your analysis objectives. Effective neural signal processing means picking the filter that best matches your recording, not sticking to one technique for everything.

Finally, a central image of a filter selecting one of several available filters reinforces the message.
