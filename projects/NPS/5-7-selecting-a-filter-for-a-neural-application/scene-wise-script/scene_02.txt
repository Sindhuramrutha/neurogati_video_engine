Subtopic: 5.7.3 Principles of Filter Selection
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Selecting a filter isn't simply about grabbing the most powerful option. The filter must remove the unwanted stuff while keeping the physiological information you need for analysis. Before you choose a filter, ask yourself: What signal am I recording? What kind of noise or interference is present? Which frequency components carry the useful physiological information? And which components should be removed? Only after answering these questions can you pick an appropriate filter. The key principle to remember is: choose the filter based on the signal, not based on the software.
