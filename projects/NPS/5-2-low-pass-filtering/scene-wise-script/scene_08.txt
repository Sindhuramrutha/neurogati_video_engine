Subtopic: 5.2.9 Summary
Scene type: manim

Narration:
In this section, we introduced the low-pass filter and examined its role in neural signal processing. We learned that a low-pass filter allows low-frequency components to pass while reducing frequencies above a selected cutoff frequency. Low-pass filtering is widely used to suppress high-frequency interference and improve signal quality. However, careful selection of the cutoff frequency is essential to avoid removing useful physiological information.
