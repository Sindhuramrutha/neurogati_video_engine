Subtopic: 5.2.8 Limitations
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Although low-pass filtering is extremely useful, it must be applied carefully. If the cutoff frequency is selected too low, useful physiological information may also be removed. Excessive low-pass filtering can make the signal appear overly smooth, and it may distort rapidly changing neural events. Therefore, the cutoff frequency should always be chosen according to the characteristics of the signal being analyzed. Remember: a low-pass filter removes high-frequency components, not necessarily noise. If useful physiological information exists above the selected cutoff frequency, that information will also be attenuated. Filter selection should always be guided by the characteristics of the biological signal, rather than by convenience alone.
