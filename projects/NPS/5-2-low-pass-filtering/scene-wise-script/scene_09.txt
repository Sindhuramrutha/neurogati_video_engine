Scene type: manim

Narration:
The low-pass filter removes high-frequency components while preserving lower frequencies. But in some situations, the opposite is required. Certain recordings contain unwanted low-frequency components, such as baseline drift or slow movement artefacts, while the useful physiological information lies at higher frequencies. To address this problem, we use another fundamental filter: the high-pass filter.
