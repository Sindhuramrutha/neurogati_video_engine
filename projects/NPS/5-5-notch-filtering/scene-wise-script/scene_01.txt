Subtopic: 5.5.3 What is a Notch Filter? — Definition and analogy
Scene type: manim

Narration:
A notch filter, formally, is a filter that attenuates a very narrow range of frequencies while letting frequencies above and below that range pass with minimal loss. Think of it like listening to a song, but there's this single annoying hum constantly in the background. You wouldn't want to turn down the whole song. You'd much rather surgically remove just that irritating tone while keeping all the music intact. A notch filter works exactly like that. It targets a narrow band around some known interference frequency and leaves almost all other signal components untouched. That's why it's so useful when you know exactly what frequency the problem sits at.
