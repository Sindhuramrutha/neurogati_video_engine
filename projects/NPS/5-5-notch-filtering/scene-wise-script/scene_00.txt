Subtopic: 5.5.1 Introduction
Scene type: manim

Narration:
So far, we've looked at low pass, high pass, and band pass filters that all remove fairly broad ranges of frequencies to clean up neural recordings. But what if the interference you're trying to get rid of doesn't spread across a wide range at all? The classic case is power line interference, that hum you get at 50 hertz in many countries, or 60 hertz in others. If you tried to cut out a whole chunk of the spectrum just to kill that one spike, you'd throw away a lot of real physiological data along with it. What you really want is a filter that removes only an extremely narrow band right around that frequency and leaves everything else alone. That filter is the notch filter.
