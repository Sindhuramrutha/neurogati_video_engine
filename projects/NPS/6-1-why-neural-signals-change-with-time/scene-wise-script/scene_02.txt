Subtopic: 6.1.3 What is a Stationary Signal?
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Let's begin with a definition. A stationary signal is a signal whose statistical and spectral characteristics remain essentially constant over time. Imagine recording the continuous hum of an electrical generator operating under stable conditions. If you observe the signal for one second, five seconds, or even one minute, its overall characteristics remain largely unchanged. The average amplitude remains similar, the frequency content remains similar, and the statistical properties remain relatively constant. This type of signal is called stationary, because its behaviour does not change significantly with time. Many classical signal processing techniques were originally developed under this assumption.
