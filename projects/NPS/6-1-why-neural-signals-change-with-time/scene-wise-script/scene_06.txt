Subtopic: 6.1.5 Why Are Neural Signals Nonstationary? — Cognitive Processes
Scene type: manim

Narration:
A third factor is cognitive processes. Attention, memory, decision-making, and learning all involve dynamic patterns of neural activity. These cognitive processes evolve continuously and influence the recorded signal.
