Subtopic: 6.1.6 An Example Using EEG
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Let's make this concrete with a thirty-second EEG recording. During the first ten seconds, the participant sits quietly with their eyes closed, and alpha activity is prominent. At ten seconds, the participant opens their eyes, and the alpha rhythm decreases. At twenty seconds, a visual stimulus is presented, and a brief event-related response appears. Although all three periods belong to the same recording, their frequency content is clearly different. This demonstrates that neural activity evolves continuously, rather than remaining constant throughout the recording.
