Subtopic: Transition to Section 6.2
Scene type: manim

Narration:
We now understand the problem. Neural signals change continuously over time, but the conventional Fourier Transform provides only a single frequency spectrum for the entire recording. So how can we determine which frequencies are present at different moments in time? The solution is the Short-Time Fourier Transform, or STFT, which we will study in the next section.
