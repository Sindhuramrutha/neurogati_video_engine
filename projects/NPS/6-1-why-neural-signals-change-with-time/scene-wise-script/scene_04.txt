Subtopic: 6.1.5 Why Are Neural Signals Nonstationary? — Behaviour
Scene type: manim

Narration:
So why are neural signals nonstationary? Neural signals reflect ongoing physiological activity within the nervous system. Since brain activity continuously changes in response to both internal and external events, neural recordings are naturally nonstationary. Several factors contribute to these temporal changes. The first is changes in behaviour. Neural activity differs depending on whether a person is resting, walking, speaking, or performing a task. As behaviour changes, the recorded signal also changes.
