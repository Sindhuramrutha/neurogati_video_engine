Subtopic: 6.1.5 Why Are Neural Signals Nonstationary? — Sensory Stimulation
Scene type: manim

Narration:
A second factor is sensory stimulation. Visual, auditory, or tactile stimuli produce transient neural responses. These responses occur only for short periods of time, and therefore introduce time-varying changes into the recording.

Animation description:
Three small icons are drawn in succession along the top: an eye labelled 'Visual', an ear labelled 'Auditory', and a hand labelled 'Tactile'. Below each icon, a baseline neural trace is shown. As each stimulus icon is highlighted, a brief transient deflection appears in the corresponding waveform — a short sharp bump that appears and then quickly fades back to baseline.
