Subtopic: 5.1.6 Important Considerations
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Filtering should always be performed carefully. A filter that is too aggressive may remove important physiological information along with the unwanted noise. Conversely, a filter that is too weak may fail to remove sufficient interference. So selecting an appropriate filter requires an understanding of both the signal being recorded and the type of unwanted components present in the recording. This is why different neural recording applications often use different filtering strategies.
