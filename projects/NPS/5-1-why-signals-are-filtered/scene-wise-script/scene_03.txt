Subtopic: 5.1.4 Why Do Neural Signals Need Filtering?
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Why do neural signals need filtering in particular? Neural recordings are highly susceptible to contamination because biological signals are extremely small. Even minor electrical disturbances can produce voltage changes that are comparable to, or even larger than, the neural signal itself. Some common reasons for filtering include: removing slow baseline drift, reducing high-frequency electrical noise, eliminating power-line interference, improving the signal-to-noise ratio, and preparing signals for feature extraction and analysis. Filtering therefore serves as an essential preprocessing step before most signal-processing techniques are applied.
