Subtopic: Applications in Neural Signal Processing
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Wavelet analysis has become an important tool in modern neuroscience because many neural signals are highly nonstationary. Some common applications include: EEG analysis, for detecting transient oscillatory activity and analysing brain rhythms that change rapidly over time. Event-related brain activity, for studying neural responses immediately before and after sensory or cognitive events. Epilepsy research, for identifying short-duration epileptic discharges that may be difficult to detect using conventional frequency analysis. And brain–computer interfaces, for extracting time-varying features from EEG signals for classification and control applications.
