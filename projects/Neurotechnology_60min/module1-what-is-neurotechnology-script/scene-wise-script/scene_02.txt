Subtopic: What is Neurotechnology?
Scene type: manim

Narration:
Let's build a working definition. Neurotechnology comprises technologies designed to interact with the nervous system to measure, interpret, influence, or restore neural function. There are several important words in this definition, so let's look at each one. First is measure. A technology may measure electrical activity, magnetic fields, physiological activity, or changes in brain structure or function. Second is interpret. Recording a signal is only the beginning. We often need signal processing and computational methods to extract information that is meaningful. Third is influence. Some technologies do not simply observe the nervous system. They deliberately change neural activity through electrical, magnetic, or other forms of stimulation. Finally, neurotechnology can help restore function. Neuroprosthetic and assistive systems can use information from the nervous system to compensate for impaired communication or movement. So whenever we think about neurotechnology, we should always ask three questions: what part of the nervous system are we interacting with? What information or effect are we trying to obtain? And what happens after that interaction?
