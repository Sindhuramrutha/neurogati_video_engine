Here we’re taking on the challenge of demystifying the brain—an organ that still stands as one of science’s most complex and fascinating frontiers. The idea is that when we combine biological insight with advances in technology, we can finally start to unlock how cognitive processing actually works. To do that, we’ll look first at cognitive complexity, which involves understanding the segmented way our thought processes operate, and then at technological integration—specifically, how robotics and AI give us models for neural function.
